> Bridging Embodied AI, Control Engineering, and Agent Systems.

I'm an undergraduate student majoring in **Mechatronics Engineering**, currently focusing on **Embodied AI, Robotics, Control Engineering, and Agent Systems**.

I enjoy turning theoretical mathematics and control algorithms into systems that actually work — from embedded hardware (ESP32/sensors) and spatial computer vision to reliable multi-agent workflows and runtime tooling.

When I'm away from coursework and the workbench, you'll probably find me running 🏃, deriving control theory & mathematical models on paper 📐, or tinkering with microcontrollers and mechanical gadgets 🛠️. The harmony of physical dynamics and clean code is what makes exploring technology fascinating.

* 🤖 Exploring **Embodied AI, Robot Manipulation & Physical Intelligence**
* ⚙️ Researching **Modern Control Theory, State Estimation & Trajectory Optimization**
* 🛠️ Building **Hardware-in-the-loop Dashboards, Agentic DevTools & Interactive UIs**
* 🧩 Contributing to **Open-Source Agent Runtimes & Reliability Engineering**
* 🔨 Always learning and tinkering at the intersection of bits, atoms, and math

> Learn. Model. Build. Repeat.
